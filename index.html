Open an existing experience

A customer or venue can import a previously customized show. No subscription or account needed for this local prototype.

How to launch: Open Experience Screen. On the new screen, tap Begin to unlock music and voice, and Full Screen for presentation. If the device has a TV or projector connected as a second display, drag the experience window there. For mapping, adjust surface corners while looking at the physical projection.
Prototype limitations: This version does not host personalized QR links, process payments, automatically scan room geometry, or synchronize multiple remote devices. The show runs locally; exporting a playable show creates a self-contained HTML file. Projection onto physical objects requires suitable hardware.
