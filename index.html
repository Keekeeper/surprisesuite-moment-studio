To use a real projector

Connect a laptop to your projector (HDMI / USB-C or compatible wireless display), point the projector at a white surface, open Projection Stage, then align the corners in Step 02. This app does not wirelessly turn a TV into a projector.

Open an existing experience

A customer or venue can import a previously customized show. No subscription or account needed for this local prototype.

iPhone / TV: First use iPhone Control Center → Screen Mirroring → select an AirPlay-compatible TV. Then press TV / Screen Mirror. The experience opens in this same tab; tap Begin. Turn your phone sideways for a wider view. Projector: Choose Projection Mapping output and use Open Experience Screen. On a laptop, move the show window onto the projector display; on a phone, the experience opens in this same tab. A TV mirrors a screen but does not project onto physical objects.
Prototype limitations: This version does not host personalized QR links, process payments, automatically scan room geometry, or synchronize multiple remote devices. The show runs locally; exporting a playable show creates a self-contained HTML file. Projection onto physical objects requires suitable hardware.
